About

Our Position

Traditional addiction treatment was built around the idea that the person needs to be broken down before they can be rebuilt. Groups, confessions, sponsors, vulnerability. For some, it works.

For a significant group of men — educated, high-earning, high-functioning — it creates more barriers than it removes. The idea of sitting in a circle, of disclosure, of the identity shift that comes with "being an addict"… it's not a path they'll take.

"The problem isn't that they're unwilling. It's that the available solutions require them to become someone they are not."

Control Protocol was designed from the opposite direction. No identity demolition. No vulnerability requirement. No public acknowledgment. Instead: a private, structured system that treats behavioral control as an engineering problem — and solves it accordingly.

The results speak for themselves. This is not a philosophy. It is a system. And it works.
